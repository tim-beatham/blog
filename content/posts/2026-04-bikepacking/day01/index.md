# Day 01: Amsterdam to Utrecht

This is my first post of the cycling trip as I speak I'm typing this in a tent
in the outskirts of Utrecht (with Nvim and Termux). I was worried that I was going to be too cold
tonight so I've wrapped up with two jumpers.

Today we've disembarked from the ferry at the DFDS Amsterdam ferry terminal;
cycled to Amsterdam and got a burger; got a coffee and a cake at Weesp; cycled
to Utrecht along the Vecht canal.

We've cycled ~60km today and we are doing the same tomorrow to get to Arnhem.
At Arnhem we will meet the EuroVelo 15.

I didn't do an update yesterday on the ferry, so this entry will combine
'zeroth' and first day into a single entry.

## Boarding the Ferry

We arrived to Newcastle by train and got in at 13:52. Then we went along the
National Cycle Route 72 to the ferry terminal in North Shields.

![Newcastle Ferry Terminal](./2026-04-20-newcastle.jpg)

On the ferry it was one big 'cheese fest', with karaoke, bingo and a middle-aged
live band -- it was an absolute joy.

We had a spare a €5 and doubled our fiver on the 'black and red' machine.

## Morning: Disembarking from the Ferry

We disembarked at ~10:00 CET. From the ferry we headed straight to Amsterdam. My
first impression of the Netherlands was that the infrastructure was amazing!
There are: bike lanes along every road we've been in, traffic lights just for
bikes and arrows that indicate who has right of way.

We noticed that the infrastructure was incredibly impressive. Within the
industrial outskirts of West Amsterdam we cycled past immense turbines
producing electricity. I felt it made the industrial complex more harmonious.

We got into Amsterdam at 12:30 where we immediately got a burger.
![Windturbines Amsterdam](2026-04-21-wind-turbines.jpg)
(Windturbines on the way to Amsterdam)

![Amsterdam Centre](2026-04-21-amsterdam.jpg)
(Cycling through Amsterdam Centre)

## Afternoon: Cycling to Utrecht

After that we promptly left Amsterdam to travel to Utrecht. Again, the cycling
infrastructure was immense. The whole route was along a huge canal and there
were canal boats carrying cargo.

We made a pitstop at Weesp for Coffee and a cake, where I had the best Red
velvet cake!

We departed the town at 16:00 and made it to Utrecht by 18:00.

## Evening: Arrival in Utrecht

We arrived at our campsite and made our tent (where arguments were had over tent
setup).
