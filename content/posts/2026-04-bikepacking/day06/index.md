# Day 06: Bad Hönnigen to Koblenz

## Passing Vineyards

We left Bad Hönnigen in the morning heading for Koblenz. Along the way we passed
the first set of vineyards of the Rhine Valley. For me, this was going to be
the most exciting part of the trip. I had always wanted to go and see the region
with its many castles and vineyards.

![Vineyards on the way to Koblenz](2026-04-26-vineyards.jpg)

## Arriving in Koblenz

We reached Koblenz at around 13:00 where we had lunch at a Syrian restaurant.
I've never had the cuisine before, but it was a phenomenal experience. We both
got 'Syrian pizzas' that consisted of spinach, cheese and walnuts. We found the
restaurant when wandering through Koblenz's industrial park.

![Syrian pizza that we had in Koblenz](2026-04-26-syrian-pizza.jpg)

Afterwards, we headed for the centre of Koblenz where we had three groß beers.
Our campsite was 10km outside of Koblenz and we arrived at around 16:00.
However, as the previous campsite had no electricity we needed to head back to
Koblenz to charge our phones. We found a 'cheesy' pub with a pool table and
table fußball. We stayed here until the last bus back to the campsite. By the end
we had several beers and even a falafel wrap!
